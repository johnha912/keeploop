# Rubric — marketing and content

| Criterion | Weight | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (a promise the piece keeps) | 25 | no |
| Clarity (one idea, repeatable in one sentence) | 25 | no |
| Evidence (claims sourced and dated) | 20 | yes |
| Voice fit (written for one named reader) | 15 | no |
| Call to action (single, honest, next step) | 15 | no |

Anchors: 90+ = would publish as the reference example · 70 = publish after
minor edits · 50 = rework the weak criterion · <50 on Evidence = hard fail:
unsourced claims do not ship, however engaging the draft.

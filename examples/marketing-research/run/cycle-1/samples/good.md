# Sample: coffee brand tracker (good)

## Screener
Objective: Keep only adults who bought ground or whole-bean coffee in the last month.
Decision: Defines the base for every later table; excluded people are not interviewed.
Analysis: Incidence rate by region; quota check against the target sample.

1. [single] Have you bought ground or whole-bean coffee in the last month?
   - Yes
   - No

## Brand awareness
Objective: Measure how many category buyers know each brand without help and with help.
Decision: Whether to spend the next quarter on awareness or on conversion.
Analysis: Unaided and aided awareness per brand, with the gap (aided minus unaided).

2. [open] Which coffee brands can you think of? Please list as many as you can.
   Awareness: unaided
3. [multi] Which of these coffee brands have you heard of? Select all that apply.
   Awareness: aided
   - Brand A
   - Brand B
   - Brand C
   - Other (specify)
   - None of these

## Satisfaction
Objective: Learn how current buyers rate their main brand on taste.
Decision: Whether to reformulate the blend or keep it and change the message.
Analysis: Top-2-box and mean by brand, cross-tabbed by region.

4. [scale] How satisfied are you with the taste of your main coffee brand?
   Scale: Very dissatisfied | Dissatisfied | Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied | Satisfied | Very satisfied
5. [grid] How satisfied are you with each of these aspects of your main brand?
   Scale: Very dissatisfied | Dissatisfied | Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied | Satisfied | Very satisfied
   - Price
   - Packaging
   - Availability

## Demographics
Objective: Describe respondents so results can be cut by age and household size.
Decision: Which audience segment the next campaign targets.
Analysis: Cross-tabs of awareness and satisfaction by age band.

6. [single] What is your age?
   - 18-24
   - 25-34
   - 35-44
   - 45-54
   - 55+
   - Prefer not to say

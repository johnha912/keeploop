# Fieldnote Apparel: Gen Z and Millennial apparel shopper study, US and Canada (first draft)

> Online, mobile first, target length 10 minutes. Brand lists are shown in random order. Forever 21 is shown as an online brand in the US.

## Screener
Objective: Qualify adults 18-45 in the US and Canada who bought clothing from a trend-led or fast-fashion brand in the past 6 months, and fill country and generation quotas.
Decision: Confirms the sample represents the target shopper for the 2027 US and Canada growth plan.
Analysis: Quota monitoring by country (US / Canada) and generation (Gen Z 18-29 / Millennial 30-45); incidence reporting.

Decision: Identifies the awareness-to-purchase funnel gaps Fieldnote has to close in each country.
Analysis: Aided awareness, past-6-month purchase and conversion (purchase / aware) by brand, country and generation.

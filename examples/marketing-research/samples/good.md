# Fieldnote Apparel: what Reddit talk says about five fast-fashion brands (fall 2026)

## Summary: start with the Aritzia-led segment, and treat Shein's ethics talk as the gap to avoid

Answer: Fieldnote should enter the US and Canada by targeting the Aritzia-led segment first, positioned on style and fit rather than on price, and should not copy Shein's low-price, low-trust profile.

Target segment: Segment 2, Aritzia-led, shipping/returns-minded, the largest segment at 23.4% of eligible authors (n=525, 95% CI 20.0 to 27.2).

- Evidence base: public Reddit talk from general fashion subreddits, a social-listening sample, not a survey of buyers. Read every share as a share of people who post.
- Shein draws the most negative talk of the five core brands, and its ethics talk is the main gap (module 5).
- Limits come after the findings, in the last section.

## 1. Top-of-mind awareness: Uniqlo is named first far more often than the other four core brands

Question: Which brands do people name first when asked where to shop?
Decision: Which competitors Fieldnote must outdo on salience in its launch markets.

- Uniqlo: 42.4% name it first (n=217, 95% CI 36.0 to 49.0), a proxy for salience.
- Zara: 3.2% (n=217, 95% CI 1.6 to 6.5), a proxy for salience.
- H&M: 1.8% (n=217, 95% CI 0.7 to 4.6), a proxy for salience.
- Aritzia: 0.9% (n=217, 95% CI 0.3 to 3.3), a proxy for salience.
- Shein: 0.9% (n=217, 95% CI 0.3 to 3.3), a proxy for salience.

The replies come from recommendation threads that lean to menswear, so the Uniqlo lead cannot be read as the women's market.

So what: Only Uniqlo stands out on this proxy; the other four core brands are inside overlapping intervals.
Recommendation: Fieldnote should not spend launch budget chasing salience against Uniqlo; it should test salience with a fielded survey first.

## 2. Brand penetration: the five core brands cannot be told apart on owning or buying talk

Question: What share of brand-mentioning authors say they own or bought each brand?
Decision: Whether any core brand has a trial or ownership lead Fieldnote must win customers from.

- H&M: 2.3% say they own or bought it (n=1453, 95% CI 1.7 to 3.3), a proxy for penetration.
- Zara: 2.3% (n=1453, 95% CI 1.6 to 3.2), a proxy for penetration.
- Uniqlo: 2.3% (n=1453, 95% CI 1.6 to 3.2), a proxy for penetration.
- Shein: 2.0% (n=1453, 95% CI 1.4 to 2.9), a proxy for penetration.
- Aritzia: 1.7% (n=1453, 95% CI 1.2 to 2.5), a proxy for penetration.

So what: The intervals overlap, so the data shows no penetration leader; it counts people who post, not buyers.
Recommendation: Fieldnote should not claim a competitor is under-owned from this data and should size penetration with a survey.

## 3. Consumer profiling: few authors state their age, and the Gen Z and Millennial shares overlap

Question: Who is talking, by self-reported age, gender and country?
Decision: Whether the talk comes from the Gen Z and Millennial shoppers Fieldnote wants.

- Age was self-reported by 2.3% of brand-mentioning authors (n=1453, 95% CI 1.6 to 3.2).
- Among them, Gen Z (14-29) is 57.6% (n=33, 95% CI 40.8 to 72.8) and Millennial (30-45) is 39.4% (n=33, 95% CI 24.7 to 56.3).
- Gender and country: base too small (13 and 21 authors), so no share is shown.

So what: The few who state their age fit the target group, but the intervals are wide and overlap.
Recommendation: Fieldnote should treat the profile as a hint only and collect age, gender and country in its own survey.

## 4. Brand positioning: Shein is talked about for quality and ethics, Uniqlo for style

Question: Which attributes is each brand talked about for?
Decision: Which attribute Fieldnote should own against Aritzia, Zara and the rest.

Counts of single-brand sentences by attribute (1100 sentence-attribute hits in total):
- Aritzia: 28 on price/value, 21 on style/trend.
- Zara: 70 on price/value, 54 on quality/durability.
- H&M: 38 on price/value, 31 on style/trend.
- Shein: 62 on quality/durability, 45 on sustainability/ethics.
- Uniqlo: 76 on style/trend, 67 on price/value.

The brand-by-attribute table differs from independence (chi-square 100.75, df 42), but hits are not independent, so read it as indicative.

Quote rule: sentences closest to the cell's median sentiment, one per author, never the most dramatic.

> "Does Shein still use child labor and exploitative practices?" (Shein, sustainability/ethics)
> "If you want to splurge (200$ range per piece), try Aritzia!" (Aritzia, price/value)

So what: Shein's talk centres on quality and ethics; Uniqlo owns style and Zara owns price talk by volume.
Recommendation: Fieldnote should position on style and fit, a space Aritzia holds only in part, and keep clear of the price fight.

## 5. Brand perception: Shein draws the most negative talk, Aritzia and Uniqlo the least

Question: How positive or negative is the talk about each brand, and on which attributes?
Decision: Which competitor weaknesses Fieldnote can exploit and which strengths to borrow.

- Shein: 23.6% negative (n=356, 95% CI 19.5 to 28.3); net sentiment 0.202.
- Zara: 18.4% negative (n=392, 95% CI 14.8 to 22.5); net sentiment 0.265.
- H&M: 10.7% negative (n=252, 95% CI 7.5 to 15.1); net sentiment 0.329.
- Uniqlo: 7.5% negative (n=504, 95% CI 5.5 to 10.2); net sentiment 0.435.
- Aritzia: 6.8% negative (n=221, 95% CI 4.2 to 10.9); net sentiment 0.412.
- Shein on sustainability/ethics: 24.4% negative (n=45, 95% CI 14.2 to 38.7).

So what: Shein's negative share is more than three times Aritzia's and the intervals do not overlap; Aritzia and Uniqlo are statistically close.
Recommendation: Fieldnote should borrow the style strength of Aritzia and Uniqlo and promote transparent sourcing against Shein.

## 6. Segmentation: five talk-pattern groups, one led by Aritzia

Question: Are there groups of authors with different brand repertoires and concerns, and which should Fieldnote target first?
Decision: Which segment Fieldnote should target first in the planned growth year.

- Segment 2, Aritzia-led, shipping/returns-minded: 23.4% of eligible authors (n=525, 95% CI 20.0 to 27.2); 6.7% of its sentiment is negative (n=299, 95% CI 4.4 to 10.1).
- Segment 3, Shein-led, sustainability/ethics-minded: 21.5% (n=525, 95% CI 18.2 to 25.2).
- Segment 4, Uniqlo-led, style/trend-minded: 19.6% (n=525, 95% CI 16.4 to 23.2).

Segments describe talk patterns, not validated shopper types; silhouette 0.189 is low.

So what: Segment 2 is the largest and the most positive, and it overlaps the brand set Fieldnote competes with.
Recommendation: Fieldnote should target Segment 2 first and validate the segment with a survey before committing budget.

## Market context (cited facts from the brief, not from the listening data)

- Hudson's Bay closed all its stores by June 2025 (Global News, 2025-06).
- Shein's US revenue fell 14.3% in Q1 2026 (Retail Gazette, 2026-07-27).

## Method and limits

Source: public Reddit posts and comments from general fashion subreddits, November 2025 to October 2026. Every brand is measured with the same dictionary, window and sources. A share is shown only for a base at or above the threshold of 30; below it the cell says base too small.

- Social listening counts people who post, not buyers. Top-of-mind and penetration are proxies.
- The sample is mostly one women's fashion subreddit plus recommendation threads that lean to menswear.
- App Store review text was unavailable, so no review sentiment is reported.
- Next study: a survey on a representative sample of Gen Z and Millennial shoppers in the US and Canada.

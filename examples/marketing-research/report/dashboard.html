The question

Which attributes is each brand talked about for?

The decision

Which attribute territory Fieldnote can claim that is not already tied to a competitor.

What we found

In plain words. z (adjusted standardized residual) is how far above or below the expected count a cell is; Bonferroni is a stricter bar for calling a cell unusual, because we test many cells at once; correspondence analysis is a map that puts brands and attributes close together when they are often talked about together.

So what

Price/value and quality/durability come up for every core brand, so they do not differentiate. Style/trend is already associated with Uniqlo, and Shein carries the ethics conversation. Fit/sizing is a lower share of Shein talk than expected, which leaves it open as a territory.

What Fieldnote should do

Fieldnote should test a trend-led, fit-reliable proposition against Zara and Uniqlo, and avoid competing on price talk, where the five brands already sit.

How we calculated this

Chi-square, adjusted residual, Bonferroni

\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E},\quad E=\frac{\mathrm{row}\cdot\mathrm{col}}{n},\quad \mathrm{df}=(R-1)(C-1)
\chi^2=100.75,\ \mathrm{df}=(7-1)(8-1)=42,\ n=1100
z=\frac{O-E}{\sqrt{E\left(1-\frac{\mathrm{row}}{n}\right)\left(1-\frac{\mathrm{col}}{n}\right)}}
\mathrm{Shein,\ sustainability/ethics}:\ O=45,\ E=21.6,\ z=6.03
|z|>\Phi^{-1}\!\left(1-\tfrac{0.05/m}{2}\right)=3.32,\ \ m=56\text{ cells}

Hits are not independent (one author can add several), so p-values are indicative.

metrics.json: (O-E)/sqrt(E*(1-row/n)*(1-col/n)); flagged |z|>1.96 and, stricter, |z|> Bonferroni z for alpha=.05/cells

Source: Haberman (1973), Biometrics 29(1):205-220 (adjusted residuals); Agresti (2002), Categorical Data Analysis; Dunn (1961), J. American Statistical Association 56(293):52-64 (Bonferroni).

Correspondence analysis

P=N/n,\quad S=D_r^{-1/2}\,(P-rc^{\top})\,D_c^{-1/2}=UDV^{\top}
\lambda_k=d_k^{2},\quad \sum_k\lambda_k=\frac{\chi^2}{n}
\frac{\chi^2}{n}=\frac{100.75}{1100}=0.0916,\quad \lambda_1=0.0702\ (76.7\%),\ \lambda_2=0.0148\ (16.1\%)

Row coordinates are U D / sqrt(r), column coordinates V D / sqrt(c); the axis percentages label the map.

metrics.json: P=N/n; r,c = margins; S=(P-rc^T)/sqrt(rc^T); S=U*D*V^T; row coords=U*D/sqrt(r); col coords=V*D/sqrt(c); axis inertia=D^2; total inertia=chi2/n

Source: Benzecri (1973), L'analyse des donnees; Greenacre (2017), Correspondence Analysis in Practice, 3rd ed., CRC Press.

The question

The decision

What we found

App Store review text could not be collected. Store-level US averages (all-time, not a sample): Aritzia 4.91, Zara 4.82, H&M 4.84, Shein 4.73, Uniqlo 4.78.

In plain words. VADER is an automatic positive/negative word scorer; kappa is agreement beyond chance.

So what

The gap in negative talk is the most defensible perception finding here, but the tool misses much criticism and the sentiment is toward sentences, not buyers.

What Fieldnote should do

Fieldnote should treat Zara's higher share of negative talk as a lead to investigate, not a proven weakness, and should measure brand attribute ratings in the survey.

How we calculated this

Net sentiment

\mathrm{net}=\frac{n_{pos}-n_{neg}}{n_{pos}+n_{neg}+n_{neu}}
\mathrm{Zara}:\ \frac{176-72}{176+72+144}=0.265

metrics.json: (pos - neg) / (pos + neg + neu), VADER compound: pos >= 0.05, neg <= -0.05, else neu

Source: Hutto and Gilbert (2014), VADER, Proc. ICWSM: compound score; positive at 0.05 or more, negative at -0.05 or less. The share-difference definition is the one recorded in metrics.json.

Cohen's kappa (VADER check)

\kappa=\frac{p_o-p_e}{1-p_e},\qquad p_e=\sum_i\frac{r_i\,c_i}{N^{2}}
p_o=\tfrac{32}{50}=0.64,\ \ p_e=0.347,\ \ \kappa=\frac{0.64-0.347}{1-0.347}=0.45

Agreement 64% (Wilson 95% CI 50.1% to 75.9%); kappa 0.45 reads as moderate. One labeller, so no inter-rater reliability.

Source: Cohen (1960), Educational and Psychological Measurement 20(1):37-46; Landis and Koch (1977), Biometrics 33(1):159-174 (bands). Not in metrics.json formulas; computed from its confusion matrix.

Wilson 95% interval

\hat p=\frac{k}{n},\qquad \mathrm{CI}_{95}=\frac{\hat p+\frac{z^2}{2n}\pm z\sqrt{\frac{\hat p(1-\hat p)}{n}+\frac{z^2}{4n^2}}}{1+\frac{z^2}{n}},\; z=1.96

Source: Wilson (1927), J. American Statistical Association 22(158):209-212. Used for every interval in this report; shown only where the base is at least 30.

Appendix

Methodology and definitions

Evidence is social listening, not a survey: public Reddit posts and comments from November 2025 to October 2026 through the Arctic Shift archive, App Store store-level ratings, and Wikipedia page views. The analysis covers 29507 Reddit items from 16697 authors; 1865 items and 1453 authors mention a brand, giving 2779 brand-mention sentences, of which 2100 name a single brand and 679 name several and are excluded from sentiment and attributes. Cross-brand modules use general subreddits only, so fan communities do not favour their own brand. Brands were found by name search with one dictionary applied to all five core brands.

Limits to keep in mind:

  • Social media is not a representative sample of North American Gen Z and Millennial shoppers; Reddit is self-selected, and who posts there is not measured in this data.
  • The collection was time-boxed and partial. The general-subreddit sample is mainly femalefashionadvice keyword search plus recommendation-thread replies, and 10 time windows were given up after retries.
  • 48 of the 60 recommendation threads are menswear, so top-of-mind results lean to menswear-friendly brands.
  • Top-of-mind, penetration and positioning are proxies, not survey measures.
  • Age, gender and country are self-reported by few authors, so generation and country splits are mostly base too small, and Canada cannot be separated from the US.
  • VADER agreement with hand labels is moderate (64%, n=50, 95% CI 50.1% to 75.9%) and it understates negativity.
  • Segments are weak (low silhouette) and describe talk patterns, not shopper types.
  • App Store review text failed to collect; only store-level ratings exist, and no review sentiment is reported.
  • No subreddit with data exists for Zara or H&M, so their within-brand context is missing.

Definitions, including the proxies

Top-of-mind (unaided awareness proxy) Base: top-level replies (one per author per thread) in selected recommendation threads that name at least one brand. First mention = the brand with the earliest position in the reply; total mention = share of those replies naming the brand (multiple response). Caveat: recommenders answer for the asker, thread selection is by reply count, and 48 of the 60 threads are menswear (sample_composition_warning), so the result leans to menswear-friendly brands. Not an awareness measure for Gen Z or Millennial women.

Penetration proxy Distinct authors with at least one ownership or purchase phrase in a sentence naming the brand ("I bought", "ordered from", "my ... from", "haul", ...) divided by distinct authors with any brand mention. A proxy, not penetration: the base is only authors who discussed brands, and phrase matching misses and over-catches. Values are small by construction.

Profiling Self-reported age ("(24F)", "I'm 24", "24 y/o", "born in 1998"; accepted range 14-65), gender ("(24F)", "as a guy", "I'm a woman"), country (first-person cues such as "I live in Toronto", "here in Canada", "as an American"). First report by an author wins. Gen Z = 14-29, Millennial = 30-45, Older = 46+ (in 2026). Coverage is reported per group; it is very low (about 2% of brand-mentioning authors give an age), so generation results rest on tiny bases and are mostly "base too small".

Positioning Brand x attribute matrix of single-brand sentences that hit an attribute keyword (a sentence can hit several). Attribute dictionaries are in dictionaries.attributes (price/value, quality/durability, fit/sizing, style/trend, sustainability/ethics, shipping/returns, customer service, store experience). Brands enter the matrix with at least 30 attribute hits. Correspondence analysis: P = N/n, S = (P - r cᵀ)/sqrt(r cᵀ), SVD of S, axis inertia = D², total inertia = chi²/n. Chi-square of independence with df = (R-1)(C-1); adjusted standardised residual = (O-E)/sqrt(E(1 - row/n)(1 - col/n)), flagged at |z| > 1.96 and separately at a Bonferroni threshold. Hits are not independent (one author can contribute several), so p-values are indicative.

Perception VADER (Hutto and Gilbert, 2014, ICWSM) compound score per single-brand sentence; positive >= 0.05, negative <= -0.05, else neutral. Net sentiment = (pos - neg) / (pos + neg + neu), with Wilson intervals on the positive and negative shares, per brand and per brand x attribute.

p_o=\tfrac{32}{50}=0.64,\ \ p_e=0.347,\ \ \kappa=\frac{0.64-0.347}{1-0.347}=0.45

Agreement 64% (Wilson 95% CI 50.1% to 75.9%); kappa 0.45 reads as moderate. One labeller, so no inter-rater reliability.

Source: Cohen (1960), Educational and Psychological Measurement 20(1):37-46; Landis and Koch (1977), Biometrics 33(1):159-174 (bands). Not in metrics.json formulas; computed from its confusion matrix.

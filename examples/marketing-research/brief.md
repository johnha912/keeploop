# Research brief: Gen Z and Millennial fast-fashion shoppers, US and Canada (fall 2026)

> Client: Fieldnote Apparel, a fictional mid-priced, trend-led apparel brand used for this
> worked example. Every other brand named here is a real competitor as of November 2026.
> This file is protected in team.yaml: the team may improve the report, never the brief.

## Business question

Fieldnote plans to grow in the US and Canada in 2027. Leadership must decide:

1. Which shopper segment to target first.
2. How to position against Aritzia, Zara and the rest of the competitive set.
3. Which brand strengths to borrow and which competitor weaknesses to exploit.

## Evidence for this report

Social listening, not a survey: public Reddit posts and comments (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026,
through the Arctic Shift archive), App Store store-level ratings, and Wikipedia page views.
The analysis output is `data/metrics.json` and `data/quotes.json`; method and limits are in
`data/README.md`. Every number in the report must come from `data/metrics.json`.

Core competitive set: Aritzia, Zara, H&M, Shein, Uniqlo.

## Modules the report must cover, in this order

1. **Top-of-mind awareness:** which brands people name first when asked where to shop.
2. **Brand penetration:** who says they bought or own each brand (a proxy).
3. **Consumer profiling:** who is talking (age, gender, country where self-reported).
4. **Brand positioning:** which attributes each brand is talked about for.
5. **Brand perception:** how positive or negative the talk is, overall and by attribute.
6. **Segmentation:** groups of people with similar brand and attribute talk, and which
   one Fieldnote should target first.

## Market context (cited, for interpretation only)

- Forever 21 closed all its US stores in 2025 and now sells in the US online and through
  wholesale only; in Canada it is run separately. (FashionUnited, 2025-09-18; Retail Insider, 2025-03-17)
- Hudson's Bay closed all its stores by June 2025; it is no longer a Canadian channel.
  (Global News, 2025-06)
- The US suspended the de minimis exemption for all countries on Aug 29 2025; Shein's US
  revenue fell 14.3% in Q1 2026. Canada kept its low-value import thresholds, and Canadian use
  of Temu and Shein rose in 2026. (Troutman, 2026-08-18; Retail Gazette, 2026-07-27;
  Retail Insider, 2026-03-12)
- Aritzia's US revenue grew 60.3% in Q2 FY27 and is now two-thirds of its total.
  (Aritzia results via newswire.ca, 2026-10-08)
- eBay completed its purchase of Depop on Jul 30 2026. (TechCrunch, 2026)

These context figures come from the cited sources, not from the listening data; the report
may quote them only in a clearly marked "Market context" section, with the source.

## Out of scope

Pricing tests, ad creative, product concepts and a fielded survey. A survey that validates
these findings on a representative sample is the recommended next study.
